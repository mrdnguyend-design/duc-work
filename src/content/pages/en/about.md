I run retention marketing and ecommerce operations across a portfolio of
direct-to-consumer brands. Day to day that means campaign and flow strategy in
Klaviyo and Postscript, bulk catalog and pricing operations in Shopify, and a
warehouse layer that lets me check whether any of it actually worked.

The part I care about most is the last one. Most retention teams are reading
numbers that are quietly wrong — a sync gap here, an attribution window there —
and building a quarter of decisions on top of them. A large share of my work is
just finding out what is true before anyone spends money.

## What I am known for

- **Retention at portfolio scale.** Multiple stores, shared playbooks, separate
  P&Ls.
- **Operations, not just marketing.** Bulk pricing ladders, catalog migrations,
  launch mechanics, and the failure modes that come with them.
- **Data that survives an audit.** Warehouse queries cross-checked against
  platform UI, because platform exports lie more often than people think.

## How I write

I publish the real numbers where I can, and the mistakes where they teach
something. There are more post-mortems here than case studies, and that is on
purpose — the incidents are where the transferable lessons live.
