I am an operator, not a consultant who has never owned a number. I run email,
SMS, and ecommerce operations across a portfolio of DTC brands, and I carry the
P&L for that work. Everything below is the same thing I do on my own stores.

## Three ways to work together

### 1. Diagnostic sprint — fixed scope, fixed fee

Two weeks. I get read access to your Shopify, ESP, SMS platform, and analytics,
then hand back a written teardown: where revenue is actually attributed, where
cost is leaking, which flows are broken, and a ranked list of fixes with
expected impact.

You keep the document whether or not we work together after it.

> Best for: brands doing meaningful revenue that suspect their retention
> numbers do not add up.

### 2. Advisory retainer — ongoing

Monthly. A standing call plus async access. I review your calendar, your flow
performance, your cost per message, and your attribution before you commit
budget. I will tell you when a campaign is not worth sending.

> Best for: teams with an in-house marketer who needs someone senior to pressure
> test decisions.

### 3. Agency and partner work

If you run an agency and your clients need retention or Shopify operations depth
you do not want to hire for, I take that work under your brand or alongside it.
Clear scope, clear boundaries, no client poaching.

> Best for: agencies strong in acquisition or creative that keep losing retention
> scope.

## Who this is not for

- Pre-revenue stores. There is nothing to diagnose yet.
- Anyone looking for someone to press send on campaigns. I am not a contractor
  for execution volume.
- Teams that want a report to justify a decision already made.

## How it starts

One call, 30 minutes, free. You describe the situation, I tell you honestly
whether I am the right person and which of the three fits. If none of them do,
I will say so.
